01. About Me

I enjoy taking fuzzy problems — ambiguous user intent, messy data, tight latency budgets — and turning them into systems that are predictable, observable, and safe to ship. My work sits between research and production: I've fine-tuned open-weight LLMs on domain tasks, built RAG pipelines that cut token costs without sacrificing answer quality, and designed evaluation loops that track semantic correctness beyond BLEU scores.
